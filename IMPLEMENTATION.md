# 3. Business Rule Implementation
 # MIM Declaration Validation
Configuration:
 . Table: Major Incident
 . When: Before
 . Insert: Enabled
 . Update: Enabled
- . Advanced: Enabled
# Responsibilities:
  . Validate declaration information.
  . Validate priority and impact/urgency combinations.
  . Prevent invalid declaration updates.
  # MIM Calculate SLA
  Configuration:
 . Table: Major Incident
 . When: Before
 . Insert: Enabled
 . Update: Enabled
 . Order: 200
# Responsibilities:
 . Read the priority value.
 . Calculate response and resolution deadlines.
 . Set the SLA status.
 . Initialize breach flags for new records.
The implementation uses GlideDateTime to calculate deadlines.

# 4. Flow Designer Implementation
Flow: MIM - Major Incident Approval

Trigger:
 . Major Incident record updated.
 . State = Accepted.
 . Approval = Not Yet Requested.
Actions:

. Ask for approval from Major Incident Manager.
. Evaluate the approval outcome.
. Retain Accepted state when approved.
. Set Rejected state when rejected.
# 5. UI Action Implementation
UI Action: Acknowledge Response

Purpose:
Allows authorized users to acknowledge the major incident response.

Implementation:

Sets Response Acknowledged to true.
Captures the acknowledgement timestamp.
Updates the Major Incident record.
Displays a confirmation message.
# 6. Scheduled SLA Monitoring
Scheduled Script Execution: MIM - SLA Breach Monitor

Schedule: Every 5 minutes.

# Responsibilities:

Identify expired response deadlines.
Identify expired resolution deadlines.
Update breach indicators.
Calculate escalation level.
Generate escalation events.
Update records while preventing unnecessary Business Rule execution.
# Server-side APIs used:

GlideRecord
GlideDateTime
gs.eventQueue()
setWorkflow(false)
# 7. Escalation Implementation
Three escalation levels are configured.

Level                                Condition                   Responsible Team





Level 1                         Response SLA breach             Team Lead

Level 2                Response remains unacknowledged 15 minutes after Level 1,   Major Incident Manager

                       without resolution breach

Level 3                Resolution SLA breach                                     IT Management

The scheduled monitor updates the escalation level and timestamp.

# 8. Event Registry
Event:

x_78515_intellig_0.mim.escalation

# Parameters:

Parm1: Escalation level.
Parm2: Major incident short description.
The event is generated by the scheduled SLA monitor when escalation increases.
